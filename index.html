Why BrainEase

Care that starts with you.

Medication guided by biology

Genetic testing and lab work inform medication choices, alongside your full clinical picture.

Lifestyle first

Sleep, nutrition and daily habits are part of the plan from day one, before and during any medication change.

One clinician, start to finish

The same person from your intro call to every follow-up. No hand-offs.

Time to listen

Visits leave room for your story, your questions and your priorities.

How it starts

Three simple steps.

  1. A free 15-minute call

    Send a request or call us. Sariette calls you back to get to know you and answer your questions. No commitment.

  2. A full psychiatric evaluation

    A comprehensive look at the biological, lifestyle and environmental factors behind what you're living with.

  3. Your plan, then follow-ups

    A plan built with you, and the same clinician at every follow-up, in person or by telehealth.

Meet Sariette

Sariette Ngandjo, APRN, PMHNP-BC

Founder of BrainEase and its only provider. A board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner, practicing with full practice authority in Illinois since 2021.

She blends evidence-based psychiatry with a functional, integrative lens, and sees children, teens and adults in English and French.
