Privacy policy.

How we handle what you share through this website. Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

When you send a request through Eze or the contact form, we collect only what you enter: your name, phone number and/or email, whether you are a new or current patient, your preferred days, time and language. We ask you not to include health information.

When you use the Eze chat, your questions are used only to answer them. Eze does not keep conversations after you leave the site.

How we use it

Only to respond to your request and schedule your visit. We do not sell or rent your information.

Newsletter

If you sign up for our newsletter, we use your email address to send it. Every email includes a link to unsubscribe.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses only what it needs to work and, if enabled, privacy-friendly visit statistics that do not identify you.

Patients

If you become a patient, your health information is protected under HIPAA. Our Notice of Privacy Practices is provided at intake and is available on request.

Contact

Questions about privacy: wecare@braineasehealth.org or 331-330-7228.
