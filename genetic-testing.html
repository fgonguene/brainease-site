Genetic testing, explained simply.

Pharmacogenomic testing looks at how your genes may affect the way your body processes certain medications. At BrainEase, it is one tool among others to help guide choices.

  1. A simple sample

    The test usually needs only a simple sample, such as a cheek swab. Sariette tells you if and when it makes sense for you.

  2. The lab looks at your genes

    A laboratory studies genes involved in how the body breaks down and responds to certain medications.

  3. Choices, made together

    Sariette reads the results alongside your history, labs and goals, and talks the options through with you.

What it can, and can't, tell you.

Genetic testing can help inform medication choices. It cannot predict on its own how you will feel, and it never replaces a full evaluation.

Will the test tell me which medication will work?

No single test can guarantee that. Results add information about how your body may process certain medications, which Sariette weighs with everything else she knows about you.

Do I need genetic testing?

Not everyone does. It is discussed during your evaluation, and the decision is made together.

Is it covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan and situation. We will help you check before any test is ordered.

Who sees my results?

Your results are part of your medical record and are protected like the rest of your health information.
