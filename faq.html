Questions, answered.

Can't find yours? Ask Eze, or call or text 331-330-7228.

How do I get started?

Send a request through Eze or the contact page, or call or text 331-330-7228. Everything starts with a free 15-minute intro call by phone.

What is the free intro call?

A brief 15-minute phone call to meet Sariette, ask your questions and see if BrainEase is the right fit. It is not a psychiatric evaluation.

Can I book online?

Not directly. You can send a request in under a minute through Eze or the contact form, with your preferred days and time. Sariette aims to get back to you within one business day.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

BCBSIL, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare / Optum and Blue Cross Community Health Plans (BCCHP). If you have another plan or no insurance, ask us about your options.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes, anywhere in Illinois, Monday to Friday. You need to be in Illinois at the time of your visit.

When can I come in person?

In-person visits are on Thursdays at 440 Quadrangle Dr., Suite I, Bolingbrook, IL 60440.

Who do you see?

Children, teens and adults from 8 to 65 years old.

Do you see patients in French?

Yes. Sariette sees patients in English and French.

How do I reschedule or change an appointment?

Email wecare@braineasehealth.org or call or text 331-330-7228.

What don't you offer?

Couples or marriage therapy, long-term psychotherapy on its own, requests outside psychiatric care, and emergency care.

What if I am in crisis?

BrainEase is not an emergency service. Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911 right away.
