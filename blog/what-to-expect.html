What to expect when you reach out to BrainEase

From your first message to your first follow-up, here is how getting started works, step by step.

By Sariette Ngandjo, APRN, PMHNP-BC · 4 min read

Reaching out about your mental health, or your child's, can feel like a big step. Here is exactly what happens when you contact BrainEase, so there are no surprises.

1. You send a request or call

You can send a request in under a minute through Eze, our assistant on the website, or through the contact page. You choose the days and the time of day that suit you. You can also call or text 331-330-7228, or email wecare@braineasehealth.org.

Please don't include health details in your request: Sariette will talk with you directly. She aims to get back to you within one business day.

2. A free 15-minute intro call

Your first conversation is a short, free phone call. It is a chance to meet Sariette, ask your questions, talk about insurance and logistics, and see whether BrainEase is the right fit. It is not a psychiatric evaluation, and there is no commitment.

3. Your psychiatric evaluation

If you decide to move forward, the next step is a comprehensive psychiatric evaluation. Sariette takes the time to understand the whole picture: your history, your daily life, sleep, nutrition, stress and what matters most to you. When useful, lab work or genetic testing may be discussed.

Good to know. Visits happen in person on Thursdays in Bolingbrook, or by telehealth anywhere in Illinois, in English or French.

4. A plan, and follow-ups with the same clinician

Together, you build a plan. Follow-up visits adjust it over time. Because Sariette is the only provider at BrainEase, you see the same person every time.

Insurance

BrainEase accepts BCBSIL, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare / Optum and Blue Cross Community Health Plans (BCCHP). If you have another plan or no insurance, ask during your intro call.
