Sariette Ngandjo, APRN, PMHNP-BC

Founder of BrainEase and its only provider. The same clinician, from your first call to every follow-up.

Sariette is a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner. She has practiced with full practice authority in Illinois since 2021 and founded BrainEase to offer the kind of care she believes in: careful, personal and grounded in evidence.

Her approach is functional and integrative. Before anything else, she wants to understand the whole person: biology, lifestyle, environment and what matters most to you. Medication decisions draw on genetic testing and lab work when they are useful, and sleep, nutrition and daily habits are part of the plan from the start.

BrainEase is intentionally small. Sariette is its only provider, so the person you meet on your first call is the person you see at every visit.

She sees children from age 8, teens and adults up to 65, in English and French, in person on Thursdays in Bolingbrook and by telehealth across Illinois.
