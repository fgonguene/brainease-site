Services built around the whole person.

Four services that work together, with the same clinician throughout.

Psychiatric evaluation

A comprehensive evaluation exploring the biological, lifestyle and environmental factors behind your symptoms, not just a checklist. It is the first clinical visit after your free intro call.

Medication management

Precise, individualized prescribing informed by your full clinical picture, lab work and, when useful, genetic testing. Follow-ups adjust the plan with you over time.

Lifestyle coaching

Support with nutrition, sleep and daily habits, woven directly into your treatment plan, before and during any medication change.

Genetic testing

Pharmacogenomic testing that looks at how your body may process certain medications, to help guide choices. How it works
